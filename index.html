Background and Rationale

Communities at the heart of conservation

As the lead ASEAN Member State on Protected Areas (PAs) for the ASEAN Working Group on Nature Conservation and Biodiversity, the Philippines is set to host the ASEAN Heritage Parks (AHP) Forum, aligning the crucial regional biodiversity agenda with the broader ASEAN 2026 Chairship theme, “Navigating Our Future, Together”.

The AHP Programme is ASEAN’s flagship initiative for biodiversity conservation, recognizing protected areas of outstanding biodiversity importance and promoting regional cooperation in their effective management. In partnership with the ASEAN Centre for Biodiversity (ACB), the AHP Secretariat, the Philippines will organize the AHP Forum as a platform for learning, collaboration, and innovation among AHP managers, community leaders, policymakers, development partners, and other stakeholders.

Distinct from the traditional ASEAN Heritage Parks Conference, which is convened every three years and focuses primarily on technical discussions, knowledge sharing, and institutional cooperation, the AHP Forum adopts a more people-centered approach by placing communities at the heart of conservation efforts.

It will highlight the critical role of AHP managers, Indigenous Peoples, local communities, women, and youth as stewards of protected areas, recognizing that long-term conservation success depends on empowered and engaged communities.

The Forum will provide a venue for AHP managers and stakeholders to contribute practical insights and recommendations toward the refinement and operationalization of the ASEAN Heritage Parks Operations Manual.
